## Title

**Tavique: Multi-Store E-commerce Template for Next.js (Fashion, Electronics, Beauty)**

## Short description (160 characters)

Three premium storefronts on one Next.js 16 + Tailwind 4 codebase. Filters, variants, cart, discounts and demo checkout. Typed, accessible, SEO-ready.

## Description

Tavique is a premium e-commerce template that gives you **three complete, distinct storefronts on one codebase**:

- **Fashion & Lifestyle:** editorial luxury with Bodoni Moda headlines, arched category tiles, a material-first hero, lookbook campaigns and a size guide.
- **Electronics & Gadgets:** search-first and technical, with spec-led product cards, a comparison table, a deals band, brand directory and a full dark theme.
- **Beauty & Personal Care:** soft and refined, with shop-by-concern tiles, shade pickers, ingredient spotlights, a one-click routine builder and journal articles.

Launch all three as a multi-brand group, or pick one with a single config change. The others drop out of the build, sitemap and search.

**A real shopping flow, not just pretty pages.**

- URL-synced filters for category, brand, price, rating, stock, size, colour, storage, material, skin type and more, with live counts and removable chips
- Weighted, accent-insensitive search with suggestions, recent searches and popular terms
- Variants with per-option stock, price and imagery: pick a colour and the gallery follows
- Quick add from any product card, with required-option validation
- Cart drawer and bag page: change size or colour in place, free-shipping progress, discount codes with clear eligibility messages, shipping methods and a tax estimate
- Checkout with full validation, an accessible error summary and a one-click "fill demo details" button
- Wishlist, recently viewed, demo account with order history and addresses

**Built to be trusted.**

- Cart totals are always re-priced from the catalogue in integer cents. The browser never decides what something costs.
- No card fields and no sensitive data in browser storage. Demo-only features are clearly labelled on screen.
- WCAG 2.2 AA target, audited with axe-core on every page and overlay (0 violations). Keyboard-friendly menus, native dialogs, visible focus and reduced-motion support.
- Static product and category pages, AVIF/WebP images, self-hosted fonts, metadata, sitemap, robots, and Product, Breadcrumb and FAQ structured data.

**Made to customise.**

- One typed config file per storefront: palette (light and dark), fonts, radii, button style, header layout, navigation, mega menus, footer, filters and homepage section order
- Products, categories and collections are plain typed data, ready to swap for Shopify, Medusa, Sanity or your own API
- 191 original illustrations that you own, plus the generator to re-render them in your brand colours
- Strict TypeScript, ESLint, 40 unit tests and documentation for customization, deployment and troubleshooting

## Honest notes for buyers

- Tavique is a **front-end template**. Payments, orders, accounts, tax and email need a commerce backend or services; see [FEATURES.md](FEATURES.md#going-live-what-to-connect).
- Demo reviews, brands and stock are fictional sample content.
- Requires Node.js 20.9+.
